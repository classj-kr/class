# Observation photographs

Downloaded and visually checked on 2026-09-22. These are actual photographs,
not generated images. No specimen features were painted in or reconstructed.
Local derivatives are orientation-normalized, resized to at most 1400 px wide,
and JPEG-compressed (mold-whole retains its original size). The viewer may crop
the display for enlargement; this is not a new microscope magnification.
